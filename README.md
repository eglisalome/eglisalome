# Salome Egli

PhD researcher at the Cambridge Cognitive Architecture Lab (CCA), University of Cambridge.
Psychologist and former industry data scientist, studying what the places we build ask of the people in them.

I run end-to-end wearables research in the wild: emergency nurses wearing eye-tracking glasses
and ECG for whole shifts, with my own tools to capture context and protect everyone in the frame.

### Tools
- **[egoanon](https://github.com/cambridge-cognitive-architecture/egoanon)**: privacy-first anonymisation of egocentric and eye-tracking video
- **[field-logger](https://github.com/cambridge-cognitive-architecture/field-logger)**: tablet app for naturalistic field studies and live observation
